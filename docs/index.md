## About Me

I am a Research Engineer at the Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT), Singapore, and a PhD researcher at Technological University Dublin, Ireland.

My research interests include wireless communication networks, IEEE 802.11 WLANs, spatial reuse, edge computing, and artificial intelligence.
